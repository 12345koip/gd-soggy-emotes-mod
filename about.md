## Soggy Emotes!

Makes your emotes soggy by adding 6 pictures of the soggy cat as options.

New emotes:
- **:soggy:**
- **:soggy_cup:**
- **:soggy_dry:**
- **:soggy_fear:**
- **:soggy_sniff:**
- **:soggy_what:**

**_Please note:_** This mod is **local**. This means that other players won't see your soggy emotes in comments unless they also have this mod installed!
